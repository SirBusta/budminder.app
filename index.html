01

Plant pages

Every plant gets a journal page tracking its stages from germination to harvest, an estimated harvest window, and a daily notebook logging water, feeding, training, measurements and notes right beside that day's photos.

02

Today

Open BudMinder and immediately see pending tasks: plants needing water or nutrients, offline sensors, and aging seed stock. Below your alerts, view each tent with its live climate readings and camera feed.

03

Environment

Chart every sensor in a tent on a unified timeline, from the past hour to custom date ranges. Click anywhere on a graph to drop an inspection line displaying what every sensor recorded at that exact moment.

Seed Vault

Track active seed inventory, batch ages and physical storage locations, with automatic warnings when a pack passes two years. Start a new grow directly from your inventory list.

05

Rate home-grown or dispensary flower on bud quality, taste, smell and effects, then tag the aromas, flavors and effects you noticed from ready-made lists.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. BudMinder is free and open source under the MIT license with no subscriptions or paid tiers.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Your journal notes, photos and sensor readings stay strictly on your local hardware. BudMinder only connects outward when you request it, such as searching public strain catalogs or reading your AC Infinity sensors.

Do I need sensors?

No. The journal, photos, strains, seed vault and smoke reports work completely without hardware. Connect sensors whenever you are ready.

What does it run on?

Any platform running Docker (including Unraid and Raspberry Pi), or as a standalone app on Windows, macOS or Linux. It uses SQLite by default, with PostgreSQL support if you prefer.

Is there a demo?

A live demo is coming soon. Until then, the screenshots on this page come from the demo's real grow, so you can see exactly what BudMinder looks like.
